# Fitness/Wellness/Salon/Spa — Review & Reputation Replies

Your Google rating is often the first thing a prospective client sees before booking — and a 1-star complaint left unanswered for a week signals something far worse than the original issue. This agent monitors your reviews across Google, Yelp, and Facebook, drafts on-brand replies in your voice for your approval, escalates service failures to the right manager with a Mindbody or Boulevard client lookup prompt, and sends you a weekly rating trend so you always know where you stand.

No auto-posting, no templated corporate-speak, and no chasing reviews manually — just a consistent, human-sounding presence that keeps your reputation clean across every platform.

---

## Who this is for

- Fitness studios, gyms, yoga and pilates centers managing multiple review platforms
- Salons, barbershops, and blow-dry bars where stylist reputation is everything
- Spas, med spas, and wellness centers where trust and tone are critical
- Multi-location owners who need location-level reputation tracking in one place

Relevant subsegments: FITN

---

## What it does

1. Monitors Google, Yelp, and Facebook daily for new reviews across all connected locations
2. Classifies every review into four urgency tiers and surfaces critical reviews (1-2 star) immediately — not at the next daily summary
3. Drafts on-brand replies calibrated to your brand voice, with two options for critical reviews; presents every draft for approval before posting
4. Escalates service failures and staff complaints to your designated manager with a prompt to pull the client's appointment record from Mindbody or Boulevard
5. Flags owner-level escalations for reviews mentioning injury, discrimination, legal threats, or high-value clients
6. Delivers a weekly rating trend digest with blended scores, review volume trends, top positive and negative themes, and a single recommended action

---

## Key integrations

- **Google Business Profile** — primary source for review monitoring and reply
- **Yelp** — monitored daily; replies drafted for owner copy-paste
- **Facebook** — business page reviews monitored and replied
- **Mindbody** — client lookup reference for escalated service complaints
- **Boulevard** — client appointment history reference for escalated complaints

## Configuration

The onboarding skill writes two artifacts:

**config.json** — stores your business name, locations, brand voice description, connected platforms, escalation contacts, and weekly digest schedule. Edit this file directly to add locations or change your escalation manager.

**## Your context in CLAUDE.md** — a plain-language summary of your business setup that the agent reads on every session. Update this section whenever your brand voice, locations, or escalation contacts change.
