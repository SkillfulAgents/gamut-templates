# Fitness/Wellness/Salon/Spa — Review & Reputation Replies

Your Google rating is the first thing a new client sees. This Gamut agent makes sure every review gets a thoughtful, on-brand reply — and that real service failures never fall through the cracks.

---

## What it is

A daily-running reputation agent that monitors your Google, Yelp, and Facebook reviews, drafts warm and personal replies in your business's voice, escalates service complaints to the right manager (with appointment context pulled from Mindbody or Boulevard), and delivers a weekly rating trend so you always know where you stand.

---

## Who it is for

Fitness studios, yoga studios, gyms, salons, spas, and wellness centers whose reputation is built on personal experience and word-of-mouth — and who are losing new clients to unanswered reviews.

If your 4.6 is quietly drifting toward 4.3 because a handful of complaints went unanswered, this agent is built for you.

**Relevant subsegments: FITN**

---

## What it does

1. Polls Google Business Profile, Yelp, and Facebook Reviews daily for new reviews across all your locations.
2. Classifies each review as positive (4-5 stars), neutral (3 stars), or negative (1-2 stars).
3. Flags negative reviews mentioning specific service failures — rude staff, injury, botched services, billing disputes — for immediate escalation to your manager.
4. Cross-references complaints with Mindbody or Boulevard appointment records so your manager has the full visit context before responding.
5. Drafts a personalized, warm reply for every new review using your brand voice — no boilerplate, no generic "Thank you for your feedback."
6. Presents all reply drafts to you for approval before anything is posted.
7. Delivers a Monday morning digest: new reviews by platform, rating trend vs. prior week, unresolved escalations, and top positive and negative themes.

---

## Key integrations

- **Google Business Profile** — primary review monitoring
- **Yelp** — high-intent local discovery platform
- **Facebook Reviews** — community trust signal
- **Mindbody** — appointment cross-reference for complaint context
- **Boulevard** — appointment cross-reference for salon/spa complaint context
- **Email / Slack** — escalation alerts and weekly digest delivery

## Getting started

1. **Import this workspace** into your Gamut environment.
2. **Run the `agent-onboarding` skill** — the agent will ask you a short set of questions about your business, review platforms, brand voice, escalation contacts, and digest preferences.
3. **Send your first prompt** — try: *"Check for any new reviews this week and show me the drafts."*

---

## Configuration

All configuration is captured during onboarding and written to the `## Your context` section of `CLAUDE.md` and to `config.json`. You can re-run onboarding at any time to update your brand voice, escalation contacts, or digest schedule.

Key settings captured during onboarding:
- Business name, type, and locations
- Active review platforms and connection status
- Brand voice description and sign-off phrase
- Topics and details never to mention publicly
- Escalation contact (name, email or Slack handle)
- Mindbody or Boulevard connection status
- Weekly digest day, time, and delivery channel
