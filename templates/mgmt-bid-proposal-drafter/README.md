# Management Consulting - Bid / Proposal Drafter

Stop rebuilding proposals from a blank document. This Gamut agent takes an inbound RFP, SOW request, or pitch brief and produces a structured first-pass consulting proposal - pulling past engagement references, team bios, and methodology assets from the firm's PSA and Salesforce - so the engagement lead can finalize a compelling submission without the manual scramble.

---

## Who this is for

Management consulting, strategy, and advisory firms that regularly respond to client RFPs or pitch for new engagements and want a faster, more consistent proposal process.

- General management consulting firms (strategy, operations, digital, change management)
- Boutique advisory firms specializing in a specific sector or function
- Proposal teams and business development coordinators managing high RFP volume
- Engagement managers and partners who spend hours on proposals that could take minutes

**Relevant subsegments: MGMT**

---

## What it does

1. Parses inbound RFPs, SOW requests, and pitch briefs - extracts client name, engagement type, scope, deliverables, timeline, evaluation criteria, and submission deadline - and flags gaps immediately.
2. Searches the PSA system and Salesforce for past engagement references filtered by engagement type and industry, formats the top references into a ready-to-insert experience table, and flags references that still need client approval.
3. Identifies the proposed team structure, pulls current bios from the bio library, and flags outdated bios or team members with thin relevant experience for this specific opportunity.
4. Drafts the proposed engagement approach in phased format: Discovery, Analysis/Design, Implementation/Roadmap, and Transition - tailored to the specific engagement type.
5. Drafts fee structure options: time-and-materials with an hours estimate per phase, and fixed-fee with stated assumptions and out-of-scope triggers.
6. Produces a complete first-pass proposal covering executive summary, situation and opportunity, proposed approach, relevant experience, team, fee proposal, and why-us differentiation.
7. Delivers a prioritized missing-information checklist categorized as submission-blocking, proposal-critical, or quality-improving.

---

## What you need to set up

- PSA system (Salesforce PSA, Kantata, Mavenlink, Deltek, or similar) connected to this Gamut workspace, or ability to export past engagement data
- Salesforce CRM connected (for client relationship context and opportunity tracking)
- Team bio library location (folder path or system)
- Standard rate card by role seniority
- Slack or email channel where proposal drafts and missing-info alerts should be sent

---

## What it does not do

- Submit proposals on your behalf
- Run conflict checks (those must be done separately through the conflicts system)
- Approve fee structures that require pricing committee sign-off
- Use client references without client approval
