# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Bid / Proposal Drafter for management consulting firms. This onboarding configures the agent with your firm's systems, past engagement library, and team data so it can produce first-pass proposals without manual setup on every RFP. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Firm name, size, and practice focus**
"What is your firm's name, how many consultants does it have, and what are the primary practice areas or industries you serve? For example: digital transformation, operations, M&A advisory, finance and accounting, HR, supply chain, or a mix."

**2. PSA and CRM system**
"Which systems does your firm use to manage engagements and client data - Salesforce PSA, Kantata (formerly Mavenlink/Kimble), Deltek, Dynamics 365, or another PSA? Do you also use Salesforce or another CRM for client relationships? Are these connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide past engagement data manually?"

**3. Bio and methodology library**
"Where are your current consultant bios and any standard methodology assets stored? For example: a shared Drive folder, SharePoint, a Notion workspace, or within the PSA system. Provide the system name and folder path."

**4. Rate structure and pricing authority**
"What is your standard day-rate or hourly rate by seniority level (partner/principal, manager, senior consultant, consultant, analyst)? Do you have standard fixed-fee ranges for common engagement types? Who has authority to approve quoted fees before they go to a client?"

**5. Conflict check process**
"How does your firm check for conflicts of interest on new opportunities - is there a formal process or system, or is it handled informally by a partner? Should the proposal proceed in parallel with the conflict check, or should it be gated on clearance?"

**6. Alert routing**
"Where should proposal drafts and missing-information alerts be sent - a Slack channel, the engagement lead's email, or the business development team's inbox? Who should receive escalation alerts when a submission deadline is approaching with outstanding gaps?"
