# Restaurant/QSR - New-Location Opening

Turns a greenlit restaurant or QSR location into a tracked opening project -- permits, vendor contracts, equipment, staffing, POS setup, and pre-opening marketing -- with deadline alerts and a daily progress brief until opening day.

---

## What it does

1. **Maintains a master opening checklist** across six workstreams: permitting, vendor contracts, equipment and buildout, technology setup, staffing, and pre-opening marketing -- with owner, due date, and status for every item.
2. **Surfaces blockers before they cascade** -- flags overdue items, items due within 7 days that are not started, and anything marked blocked without an escalation note, every day.
3. **Produces a daily progress brief** showing blockers first, workstream status, completions since yesterday, and the top 3 actions for the day -- scannable in under two minutes.
4. **Guides Toast POS setup step by step** -- from hardware delivery and account configuration through menu build, kitchen display routing, payment testing, and training mode -- without skipping steps.
5. **Manages 7shifts scheduling and labor setup** -- creates the new location, defines positions and pay rates, builds the training and opening-week schedule, and flags coverage gaps before they become a problem.
6. **Activates Google Business Profile and Yelp** -- verifies listing details, uploads photos, sets the opening date, and flags any inconsistency between the two listings before opening day.
7. **Runs opening-week protocols** -- shifts to a shift-by-shift checklist in the final week, tracks soft-open milestones, and generates the opening-day brief for the district manager or owner.
8. **Escalates clearly when a human must act** -- permit rejections, non-standard vendor terms, staffing shortfalls, and technology account issues are flagged immediately with a recommended next step and a consequence date.

---

## Key integrations

- **Toast POS** - manages hardware setup, menu build, payment configuration, kitchen display routing, and online ordering for the new location
- **7shifts** - handles scheduling setup, position and pay rate configuration, training shift planning, and labor cost tracking against targets
- **Google Business Profile** - claims and verifies the listing, uploads photos, sets the opening date, and manages pre-opening updates
- **Yelp** - claims the listing, uploads photos, enables Coming Soon messaging, and connects online ordering if applicable

## Getting started

1. **Import this workspace** into Gamut as a new project for the location you are opening.

3. **Start with your first prompt** -- try: "Give me today's opening checklist status" or "What are the blockers I need to act on today?"
