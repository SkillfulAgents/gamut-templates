# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Quote / Estimate Follow-up agent for manufacturing companies. This onboarding configures the agent with your quoting system, follow-up cadence, and reporting preferences. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Company name and quoting volume**
"What is your company's name? Roughly how many RFQ responses or quotes do you send per month, and how many open quotes do you typically have outstanding at any given time?"

**2. ERP or quoting system**
"Which system holds your open quotations - Epicor, SAP Business One, JobBOSS, a standalone quoting tool, or a combination? Is it connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide an open quote export as a CSV?"

**3. Quote validity and follow-up cadence**
"What is your standard quote validity period - 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, or does it vary? How long after sending a quote do you typically follow up for the first time? When would you like a second follow-up if there is still no response?"

**4. Tooling and program quotes**
"Do your quotes ever include tooling costs - molds, dies, fixtures, or gauges? If so, how is tooling priced in the quote - as a separate line item, amortized over a quantity, or included in the part price? Do you quote annual blanket programs with a price validity period?"

**5. Voice and tone**
"How would you describe the communication style you want the agent to use when following up with customers - formal and professional, or more direct and straightforward? Provide a sample follow-up message you have sent in the past if available."

**6. Alert routing and reporting**
"Where should draft follow-up messages be sent for approval - a Slack channel, the estimator's email, or a shared inbox? Where should the weekly win-rate report be delivered - the sales manager, the estimator, or a shared channel?"
