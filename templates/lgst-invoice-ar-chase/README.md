# Logistics/Trucking/3PL - Invoice & AR Chase

Freight AR moves slow. Shippers short-pay, dispute accessorials, and sit on net-30 invoices until someone calls. This Gamut agent connects to McLeod, Turvo, or your AR aging report, tracks every open invoice against a 30/60/90/120-day ladder, drafts follow-up messages in your voice that reference the specific load number and BOL, and delivers a weekly cash digest - so the back office can close the books without spending hours hunting payments.

---

## Who this is for

Trucking carriers, freight brokers, and 3PLs who need a faster, more consistent way to collect on outstanding invoices without the owner or accounting team manually following up on every open load.

- Asset carriers with net-15 to net-60 shipper payment terms
- Freight brokers managing carrier payables and shipper AR simultaneously
- 3PLs billing for warehousing, cross-dock, and transportation on separate invoice streams
- Back-office teams managing 50 to 500+ open invoices at any given time

**Relevant subsegments: LGST**

---

## What it does

1. Pulls all open invoices daily from McLeod, Turvo, or an exported AR aging report and classifies them by aging tier: current (0-30), 30-60, 60-90, 90-120, and 120+ days past due.
2. Handles freight-specific AR complications: short-pays (logs shipper's stated reason and flags for dispute), factoring (excludes factor-owned invoices from direct outreach), quick-pay windows (flags when discount window has passed), and credit holds (flags customers at credit limit before next load is dispatched).
3. Drafts tiered follow-up messages referencing the specific load number, PRO/BOL number, and pickup/delivery dates - calibrated in tone from friendly reminder to final notice before collections.
4. Presents all drafts for review and approval before anything is sent to a shipper.
5. Escalates 90+ day balances to the owner or credit manager with recommended next actions: credit hold, collections referral, or freight claim offset.
6. Delivers a weekly cash and AR digest with open AR by tier, top open balances, payments received, short-pays outstanding, and recommended actions.

---

## What you need to set up

- McLeod Software, Turvo, or ability to export AR aging report as CSV
- Customer/shipper billing contact list (AP department name and email per customer)
- Factoring company contact and list of factored invoice numbers (if applicable)
- Credit limit thresholds per customer
- Owner or accounting team voice and tone preferences for outreach messages
- Slack or email channel where drafts and weekly digest should be delivered

---

## What it does not do

- Auto-send follow-up messages to shippers (all drafts require approval)
- Resolve freight claims or disputes (flags them for human resolution)
- Pull live FMCSA or carrier data
