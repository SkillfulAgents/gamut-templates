# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Invoice & AR Chase agent for logistics, trucking, and 3PL companies. This onboarding configures the agent with your company's AR system, customer data, and communication preferences. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Company name and invoice volume**
"What is your company's name? Roughly how many open invoices do you have outstanding at any given time, and what is your typical total open AR balance? This helps calibrate the daily pull and prioritization."

**2. AR and TMS system**
"Which system holds your open invoices and customer billing data - McLeod Software, Turvo, QuickBooks, another accounting system, or a combination? Is it connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide AR aging reports as CSV exports?"

**3. Payment terms and credit limits**
"What are your standard payment terms with shippers - net 15, net 30, net 45, or does it vary by customer? Do you have formal credit limits per customer? If so, provide the threshold amounts or confirm they are in the TMS."

**4. Factoring arrangement**
"Does your company factor any invoices? If so, which factoring company do you use, and how are factored invoices identified in your system - a specific status flag or a separate account? This ensures the agent does not contact shippers directly on factor-owned invoices."

**5. Voice and tone**
"How would you describe the communication style you want the agent to use when following up with shippers - formal and professional, direct and matter-of-fact, or more relationship-oriented? Provide a sample message you've sent in the past if available."

**6. Alert routing**
"Where should draft follow-up messages and the weekly AR digest be delivered - a Slack channel, the owner's email, or the accounting team's inbox? Who should receive immediate alerts when a customer is recommended for a credit hold or collections referral?"
