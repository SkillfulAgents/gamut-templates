# Law Firm - Bid / Proposal Drafter

Turn an inbound RFP, panel invitation, or pitch request into a structured first-pass proposal package - pulling relevant matter references, attorney bios, and rate structures from iManage and Clio - with a prioritized missing-information checklist so the responsible partner can finalize and submit without starting from scratch.

---

## Who this is for

Law firms of any size that respond to client RFPs, participate in panel competitions, or pitch for new engagements and want a faster, more consistent proposal process.

- Am Law 200 and regional firms responding to corporate panel RFPs
- Boutique and specialty firms pitching for matter-specific engagements
- Business development teams coordinating multi-practice proposals
- Partners who spend hours assembling proposals that could take minutes

**Relevant subsegments: LAWF**

---

## What it does

1. Parses inbound RFPs, panel briefs, or pitch requests and extracts all key fields - prospect name, practice areas, submission deadline, required sections, page limits, and evaluation criteria - then flags gaps immediately.
2. Searches iManage or Clio for relevant past and current matter references filtered by practice area, industry, and jurisdiction, formats the top references into a ready-to-insert experience table, and flags references that still need client approval.
3. Identifies the proposed team structure, pulls current attorney bios, and flags outdated bios or team members with thin relevant experience for this specific pitch.
4. Drafts fee and rate structure options - hourly with blended rates, flat fee, capped fee, or phased AFAs - with budget estimate ranges based on comparable historical matters.
5. Produces a complete first-pass draft of all RFP sections: executive summary, firm overview, experience, proposed team, approach, fee proposal, technology/innovation, and references.
6. Delivers a prioritized missing-information checklist categorized as submission-blocking, proposal-critical, or quality-improving, with a responsible person and deadline for each item.
7. Packages the full draft and a companion summary sheet into iManage or Clio for partner review before submission.

---

## What you need to set up

- iManage or Clio connected to this Gamut workspace (read access to matters and document libraries)
- Attorney bio library location (folder path in iManage or Clio, or firm website)
- Rate card or standard hourly rates for each seniority level
- Standard proposal template or past winning proposals to use as style references
- Slack or email channel where missing-information alerts should be sent

---

## What it does not do

- Submit proposals on your behalf
- Run conflict checks (that must be done separately through the conflicts system)
- Approve fee structures that require pricing committee sign-off
- Use matter references without client approval
