# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Bid / Proposal Drafter for law firms. This onboarding configures the agent with your firm's systems, credentials, and standards so it can produce accurate first-pass pitch packages without manual setup on every RFP. Ask the following questions in order, then save the config and update the agent's context.

---

## Questions to ask

Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding. If the user skips a question, record the value as `null` and note it as a gap.

**1. Firm name, size, and practice focus**
"What is your firm's name, how many attorneys does it have, and what are the primary practice areas you pitch for most often? For example: corporate M&A, commercial litigation, employment, real estate, regulatory, or a mix?"

**2. Document and matter management system**
"Which system does your firm use to store matters and documents - iManage, Clio, NetDocuments, or another system? Is it connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide matter information manually?"

**3. Bio and proposal template storage**
"Where are your current attorney bios and any standard proposal templates stored? For example: a specific iManage workspace, a SharePoint folder, a firm intranet, or a shared drive. Provide the system name and folder path."

**4. Rate card and fee authority**
"What is the standard hourly rate range by seniority level (partner, senior associate, associate, paralegal)? Are there approved AFA structures you use regularly - flat fees, capped fees, phased engagements? Who has authority to approve quoted rates before they go to a client?"

**5. Conflict clearance process**
"How does your firm run conflict checks - which system or team handles them? Should the proposal draft be gated on conflict clearance, or should it proceed in parallel while conflicts are cleared?"

**6. Alert channel for missing information**
"Where should the agent send missing-information alerts and draft proposals for review - a specific Slack channel, an email address, or directly to the responsible partner? Provide the channel or email."
