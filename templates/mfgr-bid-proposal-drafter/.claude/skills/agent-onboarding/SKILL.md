# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Bid / Proposal Drafter for manufacturing companies. This onboarding configures the agent with your production capabilities, ERP system, and quoting standards so it can produce accurate first-pass proposals without manual setup on every RFQ. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Company name, facility, and primary processes**
"What is your company's legal name as it should appear on proposals? What are your primary manufacturing processes - for example: CNC machining, stamping, injection molding, casting, fabrication, welding, assembly, or a mix? What industries do you primarily serve - automotive, aerospace, defense, medical, industrial, or general manufacturing?"

**2. ERP system**
"Which ERP system does your company use - Epicor, SAP Business One, JobBOSS, Global Shop, or another system? Is it connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide cost and job history data manually (CSV export)?"

**3. Quality certifications**
"What quality certifications does your company hold? Please provide the certification name, certificate number, certifying body, and expiry date for each. Common examples: ISO 9001, IATF 16949, AS9100, NADCAP, ITAR registration, CMMC level."

**4. Standard cost structure**
"How does your company calculate quoted costs? Walk through your cost elements: material (how you price it - standard cost, market + adder, or quoted per job), labor rates by work center or machine, overhead or burden rate, and outside process markup. Provide a reference cost sheet if available."

**5. Capacity and lead time standards**
"What is your current production capacity utilization estimate? What are your standard lead times by part type or process - for example: first article in 8 weeks, production in 4 weeks after FAI approval? Are there processes where capacity is currently constrained?"

**6. Alert routing**
"Where should proposal drafts and missing-information alerts be sent - a Slack channel, the estimator's email, or a shared sales inbox? Who should receive escalation alerts when a bid deadline is approaching with outstanding gaps?"
