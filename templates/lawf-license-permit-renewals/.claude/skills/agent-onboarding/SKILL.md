# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the License / Permit / Cert Renewals agent for law firms. This onboarding configures the agent with your firm's attorney roster, credential systems, and alert preferences so it can track the full compliance stack without manual setup. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Firm name and attorney roster**
"What is your firm's name? How many attorneys does the firm have? Please provide a list of attorneys (or confirm you can export one from iManage or Clio) including their name, admitted states, and bar numbers. If you have an existing credential tracking spreadsheet, you can share it and the agent will import from it."

**2. Document management system**
"Which system does your firm use to store compliance documents - iManage, Clio, SharePoint, or another platform? Is it connected to this Gamut workspace, or will documents be managed manually? Provide the folder path where renewal confirmations should be stored."

**3. CLE tracking**
"How does your firm currently track CLE compliance - a dedicated CLE tracking platform (Compliance Anchor, CLE Tracker), a spreadsheet, or relying on individual attorneys? Do you have state-specific credit type requirements (ethics hours, technology, elimination of bias) that need to be tracked separately?"

**4. Malpractice insurance**
"Who is your malpractice carrier, what is the policy number, and when does the policy renew? Who is responsible for renewing it - the managing partner, firm administrator, or an insurance broker?"

**5. Alert routing**
"Where should renewal alerts and compliance reports be sent - a Slack channel, a firm administrator's email, or the managing partner? For lapse escalations (credential actually expired), who should be notified first?"

**6. Renewal authority**
"Who in the firm is responsible for processing bar registration renewals and malpractice renewals - a firm administrator, individual attorneys, or a combination? Should the agent send reminders directly to attorneys, to the administrator only, or both?"
