# Law Firm - License / Permit / Cert Renewals

One overlooked bar registration. One missed CLE deadline. One lapsed malpractice COI. Law firms carry a dense credential stack across every attorney and every jurisdiction they practice in - and tracking it in a spreadsheet that only gets opened after something slips is how bar complaints and coverage gaps happen. This agent keeps the full credential registry current, sends tiered renewal nudges well before anything lapses, and surfaces attorney-level compliance status on demand.

---

## Who this is for

Law firms that need to track attorney bar admissions, CLE compliance, malpractice insurance, and firm entity registrations across multiple jurisdictions - without relying on manual spreadsheets or individual attorney memory.

- Am Law 200 and regional firms with attorneys admitted in multiple states
- Boutique and specialty firms with complex CLE credit-type requirements
- Law firm administrators and office managers responsible for compliance oversight
- Managing partners who need audit-ready credential status reports

**Relevant subsegments: LAWF**

---

## What it does

1. Maintains a structured credential registry for every attorney (bar admissions by state, CLE status, pro hac vice admissions, specialty certifications) and for the firm entity (malpractice COI, state registrations, IOLTA accounts, e-filing credentials).
2. Monitors expiry dates daily and sends tiered renewal reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 14 days out - with escalating urgency, renewal portal links, and fee amounts where available.
3. Tracks CLE compliance in real time - credits completed vs. required, broken down by credit type (general, ethics, specialty) - and alerts attorneys who are behind pace before the cycle closes.
4. Immediately escalates lapses to the managing partner and firm administrator, flags active matters in affected jurisdictions for coverage review, and tracks reinstatement requirements.
5. Logs every renewal action (date, new expiry, confirmation number, amount paid) and stores renewal documents in iManage or Clio for audit and malpractice defense purposes.
6. Generates an audit-ready compliance summary by attorney and by entity on demand or on a configured schedule.

---

## What you need to set up

- iManage or Clio connected to this Gamut workspace (for document storage and matter lookup)
- List of all attorneys, their admitted jurisdictions, and their bar numbers
- CLE tracking system or manual credit records by attorney
- Malpractice insurance carrier and policy details
- Slack or email channel where renewal alerts and lapse escalations should be sent
- Firm administrator or office manager who receives escalated alerts

---

## What it does not do

- Submit bar registrations or CLE course completions on your behalf
- Enroll attorneys in CLE courses
- Make coverage determinations about matters when an attorney's credential lapses
