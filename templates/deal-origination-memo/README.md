# Deal Origination & Memo — Gamut Agent Template

Turn your firm's investment thesis or M&A mandate into a sourced, enriched target list with outreach hooks, draft IC memos and deal summaries from research, and track open diligence requests — all in one agent.

**Relevant subsegments: PEVC, IBMA**

---

## What this agent does

- **Target list sourcing** — Takes a thesis or mandate brief and conducts structured research across PitchBook, Capital IQ, Grata, LinkedIn, and the web to surface a prioritized list of companies with sector, size signal, ownership structure, key contacts, and origination rationale.
- **Enrichment & outreach hooks** — Summarizes recent news, financing events, and leadership changes for each target; drafts personalized first-touch outreach emails or LinkedIn messages.
- **IC memo & deal summary drafting** — Drafts investment committee memos and executive deal summaries in your firm's preferred format from research notes and uploaded materials; flags data gaps.
- **Diligence & data-room tracking** — Maintains a tracker of open diligence requests, data-room documents received vs. outstanding, and upcoming deadlines; sends Slack nudges when items are overdue.

---

## Who it's for

Private equity, venture capital, and growth equity firms, and investment banking / M&A advisory teams that want to consolidate origination research and memo-drafting work that currently lives across five or more tools.

## Getting started

1. Import this template into your Gamut workspace.
2. Run the `agent-onboarding` skill — it will ask about your firm, thesis, memo format preferences, and connect Google Drive and Slack.
3. Start with your first sourcing request.

**First task to try:**
> "Source a list of 20 HVAC distribution companies in the Southeast with $10M–$50M revenue for our buyout mandate."

---

## Customization

- **Thesis updates** — Tell the agent your updated mandate at any time; it will revise its targeting parameters.
- **Memo format** — Upload a sample memo during onboarding or paste your section headings directly; the agent will follow your firm's structure.
- **CRM sync** — After onboarding, ask the agent to log new targets or update deal stages directly in your CRM.
