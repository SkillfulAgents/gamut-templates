# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the License / Permit / Cert Renewals agent for trucking, logistics, and 3PL companies. This onboarding configures the agent with your fleet, driver roster, and compliance systems. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Company name, DOT number, and fleet size**
"What is your company's legal name, USDOT number, and MC authority number (if applicable)? How many power units does your fleet have, and how many CDL drivers do you employ? This determines the UCR fee tier and shapes the compliance registry."

**2. Driver roster and CDL data**
"Can you provide a driver roster with each driver's name, CDL state and number, CDL class and endorsements, and DOT physical (medical card) expiry date? If you have an existing compliance spreadsheet or a TMS export, share it and the agent will import from it."

**3. Vehicle fleet and inspection records**
"Can you provide a vehicle list with each unit's VIN, plate number, state, IRP plate expiry, and last annual DOT inspection date? Include both power units and trailers if trailers are company-owned."

**4. Regulatory programs active**
"Which of the following apply to your operation: IFTA (interstate fuel tax), IRP (apportioned registration), UCR (unified carrier registration), hazmat registration with PHMSA, CTPAT certification, TWIC cards for drivers, brokerage authority (BMC-84 bond)? Check all that apply."

**5. Compliance and safety management**
"Who is your safety director or fleet manager responsible for compliance? Is your compliance tracked in McLeod, Samsara, a dedicated ELD/compliance platform (J.J. Keller, Motus, Lytx), or a spreadsheet?"

**6. Alert routing**
"Where should renewal reminders and compliance summaries be sent - a Slack channel, the safety director's email, or the owner's inbox? For lapse escalations (credential actually expired or driver OOS), who should receive the immediate alert?"
