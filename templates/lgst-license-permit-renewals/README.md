# Logistics/Trucking/3PL - License / Permit / Cert Renewals

One expired medical card. One driver pulled out of service roadside. One missed UCR deadline. Trucking carries a dense compliance stack - operating authority, IFTA, IRP, UCR, annual DOT inspections, CDL renewals, DOT physicals, hazmat registration, brokerage bonds - across every driver and every unit. Losing track of any one of them means a driver off the road, a unit OOS, or an FMCSA revocation action. This agent tracks the full stack, sends tiered renewal nudges before anything lapses, and escalates immediately when something slips.

---

## Who this is for

Trucking carriers, freight brokers, and 3PLs that need to track the full compliance stack across drivers, vehicles, and the company entity - without relying on a spreadsheet that nobody updates between renewals.

- Asset carriers (dry van, reefer, flatbed, specialized) managing CDL drivers and DOT-regulated vehicles
- Owner-operators expanding to multi-truck fleets with increasing compliance complexity
- Freight brokers tracking brokerage authority, BMC-84 bond, and UCR registration
- Safety directors and fleet managers responsible for FMCSA compliance

**Relevant subsegments: LGST**

---

## What it does

1. Maintains a compliance registry for the company entity (MC/USDOT authority, UCR, IFTA, IRP, brokerage bond, CTPAT, hazmat registration), each vehicle (annual DOT inspection, state registration, IRP plate, IFTA decal), and each driver (CDL with endorsements, DOT medical card, clearinghouse status, TWIC, hazmat TSA background check).
2. Monitors every credential daily and sends tiered renewal reminders at 90, 60, 30, and 14 days out - with renewal portal links and fee estimates where available.
3. Alerts on quarterly IFTA filing deadlines (April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31) 30 days in advance.
4. Escalates lapses immediately to the owner and safety director, flags non-deployable drivers and OOS vehicles, and tracks reinstatement steps.
5. Generates a compliance summary by driver, vehicle, and entity on demand or on a configured weekly schedule.

---

## What you need to set up

- List of all drivers with CDL numbers, states, endorsements, and DOT physical expiry dates
- List of all units with VINs, plate numbers, last annual inspection dates, and state registration expiry dates
- USDOT number and MC authority number
- UCR registration history and fleet size (for fee tier)
- IFTA license number and jurisdiction decal records
- Slack or email channel where renewal alerts and compliance summaries should be sent

---

## What it does not do

- File IFTA returns on your behalf
- Renew operating authority, IRP plates, or UCR registration automatically
- Schedule or conduct DOT inspections or driver DOT physicals
