# Logistics/Trucking/3PL - Bid / Proposal Drafter

Win more freight bids without the manual scramble. This Gamut agent takes an inbound RFP, freight RFQ, dedicated carrier request, or 3PL contract bid and produces a structured first-pass proposal - pulling lane history, equipment availability, safety records, and rate benchmarks from McLeod, Turvo, or Samsara - so the sales or ops team can finalize a competitive submission in a fraction of the time.

---

## Who this is for

Trucking carriers, freight brokers, and 3PL providers that regularly respond to shipper RFPs or lane bids and want a faster, more consistent proposal process.

- Asset-based carriers (dry van, reefer, flatbed, specialized)
- Non-asset freight brokers and 3PLs bidding on contract business
- Dedicated contract carriers responding to fleet outsourcing RFPs
- Operations managers and sales reps who spend hours assembling bids manually

**Relevant subsegments: LGST**

---

## What it does

1. Parses inbound RFPs, lane bid requests, and 3PL RFQs - extracts lane details, commodity specs, equipment requirements, service standards, rate structure requested, and submission deadline - and flags gaps immediately.
2. Pulls historical lane performance from McLeod or Turvo - shipment count, on-time delivery rate, transit time, claim rate - and flags new lanes with no history as such.
3. Confirms equipment availability and fleet capacity against the requested volume and service period, flagging constraints that affect the ability to commit.
4. Assembles the safety and compliance package: FMCSA SMS scores, CSA percentiles, DOT and MC authority, insurance certificates, and required certifications (CTPAT, ISO, Hazmat).
5. Drafts the rate structure (per-mile, all-in flat, cost-plus, or rate-per-hundredweight) with fuel surcharge schedule and full accessorial rate table.
6. Produces a complete first-pass proposal covering executive summary, company overview, lane capabilities, safety record, rate proposal, technology and visibility, and references.
7. Delivers a prioritized missing-information checklist categorized as submission-blocking, proposal-critical, or quality-improving.

---

## What you need to set up

- McLeod Software, Turvo, or Samsara connected to this Gamut workspace (or ability to export lane history and fleet data as CSV)
- Current FMCSA DOT number and MC authority number
- Current insurance certificates (cargo, general liability, auto liability) with limits
- Standard rate card or rate-per-mile benchmarks by lane and equipment type
- Fuel surcharge table tied to DOE diesel index
- Slack or email channel where proposal drafts and missing-info alerts should be sent

---

## What it does not do

- Submit proposals or bids on your behalf
- Negotiate rates with shippers
- Pull live spot market rates from load boards (Dat, Truckstop) without integration
