# Agent Onboarding

Welcome to the Bid / Proposal Drafter for logistics, trucking, and 3PL providers. This onboarding configures the agent with your company's systems, fleet data, and rate structure so it can produce accurate first-pass proposals without manual setup on every bid. Ask the following questions in order, waiting for each answer before proceeding.

---

## Questions to ask

**1. Company name, DOT/MC numbers, and service types**
"What is your company's legal name as it should appear on proposals? What is your USDOT number and MC authority number? What are your primary service types - dry van truckload, reefer, flatbed, LTL, dedicated, 3PL warehousing, or a mix?"

**2. Geographic coverage and terminals**
"What states and regions does your fleet cover regularly? Do you have terminal locations or drop yards outside your home state? List your primary service territory."

**3. TMS and fleet management systems**
"Which of the following systems does your company use? Check all that apply: McLeod Software, Turvo, Samsara, TMW, PCS TMS, another TMS. Are any of these connected to this Gamut workspace, or will you provide lane history and fleet data manually?"

**4. Rate structure and pricing authority**
"What rate structures do you typically quote - per mile, all-in flat rate, cost-plus, rate-per-hundredweight? Who has authority to approve quoted rates before they go to a shipper? Provide a standard rate range or reference rate card if available."

**5. Safety and compliance data**
"What is your current FMCSA SMS overall percentile and your Unsafe Driving, HOS, and Vehicle Maintenance percentiles? Do you hold any special certifications - CTPAT, ISO 9001, Hazmat authority, or temperature-controlled certifications?"

**6. Alert routing**
"Where should proposal drafts and missing-information alerts be sent - a Slack channel, a sales rep's email, or a shared inbox? Who should receive escalation alerts when a bid deadline is approaching with outstanding gaps?"
