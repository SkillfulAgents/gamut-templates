# Fitness/Wellness/Salon/Spa — New-Location Opening Agent

Opening a second (or fifth) location is one of the highest-stakes projects a fitness studio, salon, or spa owner takes on. This Gamut agent turns the chaos of 50+ parallel tasks into a structured, tracked, and actively managed opening project — so nothing slips and the owner's energy stays on the decisions that matter.

## Who it is for

Fitness studio, salon, spa, yoga studio, med spa, and wellness business owners who are opening a new location and need to coordinate landlord, vendors, contractors, staff, licensing, and pre-sale simultaneously — without dropping anything.

Best fit:
- Multi-location or expanding single-location businesses
- Owners who are also running existing locations during the build-out
- Operators using **Mindbody** or **Boulevard** who need system setup tracked alongside physical build-out
- Teams without a dedicated project manager for new openings

**Relevant subsegments: FITN**

## What it does

1. **Generates a phased opening checklist at kickoff** — covering site and lease, permits and licenses, build-out and equipment, software setup (Mindbody or Boulevard), hiring and training, pre-sale marketing, and opening-day operations — with deadlines and owners assigned to every item.
2. **Sends a daily progress brief** — completed items, what is due in the next 7 days, and any blocked or overdue items with recommended next actions.
3. **Fires immediate deadline alerts** — when a permit, contractor milestone, or equipment delivery is overdue, the owner hears about it right away, not the next morning.
4. **Tracks vendor and contractor milestones separately** — outstanding quotes, signed vs. unsigned contracts, and scheduled install or delivery dates in one view.
5. **Monitors pre-sale membership pace** — reports actual vs. target weekly and flags if the pace will miss the opening-day goal.
6. **Surfaces decision prompts** — proactively asks for the owner's input when the project is waiting on them (equipment order confirmation, offer letter approval, marketing sign-off).
7. **Archives a reusable opening playbook** — at project completion, packages the full checklist and timeline so the next opening starts with a head start.

## Key integrations

- **Mindbody** — tracks system setup tasks: account configuration, class/service setup, staff accounts, online booking, and payment processing readiness.
- **Boulevard** — tracks account setup, appointment book configuration, staff onboarding, and integration readiness.
- **Email / Slack / in-chat** — daily progress brief delivered to whichever channel the owner prefers.

## Getting started

1. **Import this workspace** into your Gamut environment.
2. **Run the `agent-onboarding` skill** — the agent will ask you a short set of questions about your new location, current status, key milestones, vendors, systems, and pre-sale plan.
3. **Send your first prompt** — try: *"Generate the full opening checklist for [location name] and flag the three most urgent items for this week."*

## Configuration

All configuration is written to `CLAUDE.md` and `config.json` during the onboarding conversation. You can update any field (opening date, pre-sale target, vendor contacts, preferred update channel) at any time by telling the agent directly.
