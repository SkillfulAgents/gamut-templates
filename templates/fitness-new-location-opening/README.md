# Fitness/Wellness/Salon/Spa — New-Location Opening

Opening a second (or fifth) location is the highest-stakes operational project a studio, salon, or spa owner will run — and the one most likely to slip when there's no dedicated project manager on staff. This agent acts as your opening day project manager: it builds the full phased checklist from permits to pre-sale, tracks every workstream, surfaces blockers before they become delays, and keeps you informed with a daily progress brief until the ribbon is cut.

Whether you're opening a boutique fitness studio, a hair salon, a med spa, or a yoga center, this agent gives you the same systematic rigor that multi-unit franchise operators use — without the overhead.

---

## Who this is for

- Fitness studio, gym, yoga, pilates, and barre owners adding a new location
- Salon and barbershop operators expanding to a second or third site
- Spa, med spa, and wellness center founders opening a new facility
- GMs or operations leads managing a new-location opening project

Relevant subsegments: FITN

---

## What it does

1. Runs a kickoff interview to capture your opening date, location details, staffing plan, and pre-sale goals
2. Generates a full phased opening checklist across six workstreams: permits, facility/vendors, staffing, software setup, pre-sale marketing, and final countdown
3. Assigns deadlines calculated backward from your opening date with built-in buffers for government and vendor lead times
4. Delivers a Daily Opening Brief showing overdue items, upcoming deadlines, completed tasks, and your pre-sale member count vs. goal
5. Sends deadline alerts at 7 days, 3 days, and same-day for every open task
6. Tracks Mindbody or Boulevard location setup as its own sub-project with key configuration milestones
7. Surfaces owner decision prompts with a recommended default and a deadline for deciding without impacting the opening
8. Archives the entire project as a reusable Opening Playbook — real lead times, vendor contacts, and lessons learned — when the location opens

---

## Key integrations

- **Mindbody** — tracks location configuration, staff setup, booking activation, and payment testing milestones
- **Boulevard** — tracks location setup, service menu configuration, online booking, and payment testing milestones
- Project notes, vendor contacts, and daily briefs are stored in the Gamut workspace

## Getting started

1. Import this workspace into Gamut
2. Run the `agent-onboarding` skill — type `run agent-onboarding`
3. Send your first task — for example: "We just signed the lease for our new downtown location. Opening target is September 15. Let's build the opening checklist."

## Configuration

The onboarding skill writes two artifacts:

**config.json** — stores your location details, opening date, pre-sale goal, staff owners, vendor contacts, and connected systems. Edit this file directly to update any configuration after onboarding.

**## Your context in CLAUDE.md** — a plain-language summary of your business setup that the agent reads on every session to stay oriented. You can update this section at any time with new context.
