# AEC License / Permit / Cert Renewals

Tracks every expiring license, PE stamp, COI, business permit, and continuing education requirement across the firm and its staff, sending tiered renewal nudges and maintaining an audit-ready compliance record.

---

## The problem

Architecture, engineering, and design firms manage a sprawling web of credentials: PE licenses across multiple states, AIA memberships, certificates of insurance tied to individual projects, business permits, DBE/MBE certifications, LEED credentials, and continuing education requirements with rolling deadlines. A single lapsed COI can hold up a contract. An expired PE stamp on construction documents can void a project. Most firms track this in spreadsheets that nobody owns - until something lapses.

This agent replaces that spreadsheet with an active, always-current compliance system.

---

## What it does

1. Maintains a structured registry of every license, permit, certification, and insurance certificate for the firm and all staff - with expiration dates, renewal contacts, issuing authorities, and proof documents.
2. Runs tiered expiration checks - Critical (0-30 days), Warning (31-60 days), and Upcoming (61-90 days) - and surfaces exactly what needs action and by whom.
3. Sends targeted renewal nudges to the right people at the right time: individual staff for personal credentials, insurance brokers for COIs, and principals for firm-level licenses.
4. Cross-references PE license states against active project states to catch multi-state practice gaps before they become contract problems.
5. Tracks continuing education progress separately from license expiration, flagging staff who are behind pace on PDH/LU/AIA HSW requirements.
6. Generates audit-ready reports: firm-wide compliance summaries, individual staff credential cards, project-specific insurance snapshots, and 12-month expiration calendars.
7. Integrates with Deltek Vision/Vantagepoint and BQE Core to pull staff records and project data rather than requiring manual entry of everything from scratch.
8. Handles lifecycle events: new hire credential intake, role-change credential reviews, and departure checks to catch firm licenses held in an individual's name.

---

## Key integrations

- **Deltek Vision / Vantagepoint**: Pulls employee HR records and active project data to identify staff roles, states of practice, and existing license fields already tracked in the system.
- **BQE Core**: Pulls employee profiles and certification records; syncs registry updates back when supported.
- **State licensing board portals**: Provides direct lookup URLs and license numbers for manual verification of PE license status on state engineering board public databases.
