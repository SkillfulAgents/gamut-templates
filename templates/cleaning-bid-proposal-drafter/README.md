# Cleaning/Janitorial - Bid / Proposal Drafter

Cleaning companies spend hours hand-crafting proposals for every commercial bid request — this agent drafts the first pass from your past work and flags what's missing, so you spend minutes reviewing instead of hours writing.

## Who this is for

Commercial cleaning and janitorial operators who regularly respond to RFPs and bid requests for office buildings, retail spaces, medical facilities, schools, and other commercial accounts.

**Relevant subsegments: CLEN**

Best fit for owner-operators and small teams that win commercial contracts through competitive bidding and want a consistent, professional proposal process without a dedicated sales staff.

## What it does

1. **Receives and parses inbound bids** — accepts RFPs from email, web forms, or direct messages and extracts facility type, square footage, service frequency, special requirements, and bid deadline
2. **Matches to past proposals** — pulls comparable past proposals from your connected storage and surfaces relevant scope sections, pricing precedents, and client references
3. **Drafts a first-pass proposal** — produces a structured document (executive summary, scope of work, pricing table, company overview, references, terms) and appends a missing-info checklist for anything the owner needs to fill in before sending
4. **Logs and tracks bids** — records each bid in your job tracker with status (drafted / submitted / won / lost) and sets a follow-up reminder at your configured interval
5. **Delivers a weekly win-rate digest** — bids submitted, win rate, average bid value, pipeline total, and bids approaching follow-up window

## Key integrations

- **Swept** — job management and scheduling
- **Janitorial Manager** — job tracking and operations
- **Google Drive / Dropbox** — proposal library and past work samples
- **Email** — inbound bid intake and outbound proposal delivery
- **Slack** — bid pipeline alerts and weekly digest

## Getting started

1. Import this workspace into Gamut
2. Run the `agent-onboarding` skill — it will ask for your business details and configure the agent for your operation
3. Give the agent its first task: *"Draft a proposal for a 20,000 sq ft office cleaning contract, 3x per week, with a bid due in 5 days."*

## Configuration

After onboarding, settings are stored in `.claude/skills/agent-onboarding/config.json`. You can edit that file directly to update pricing models, follow-up timing, or connected storage locations at any time.
